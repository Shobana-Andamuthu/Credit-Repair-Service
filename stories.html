DOCUMENTED CASE WORKFLOW

Medical Collection Dispute & Mortgage Readiness

PHASE 01 • INITIAL CHALLENGE

Client identified two duplicate $450 collections reported by an agency for a bill covered by insurance, depressing scores prior to a home loan.

PHASE 02 • STATUTORY FCRA AUDIT

Conducted comprehensive 3-bureau audit, gathered billing statements, and filed formal dispute requests under FCRA § 611 and FDCPA § 809.

PHASE 03 • DOCUMENTED RESOLUTION

Credit bureaus completed 30-day investigation; furnisher failed to validate records, and duplicate items were deleted from Equifax and TransUnion.
