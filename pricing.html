Consumer Rights & Compliance Notice (CROA)

Under the federal Credit Repair Organizations Act (15 U.S.C. § 1679), you have the legal right to dispute inaccurate information in your credit report by yourself directly with credit bureaus without paying any fee. Veritas Credit provides professional document analysis, dispute preparation assistance, and personalized coaching. No credit repair company can guarantee score increases or promise the removal of accurate, timely, and verifiable records. Every case is evaluated individually.
